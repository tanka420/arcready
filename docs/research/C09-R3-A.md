## 3. Evaluated source contract

R3-A evaluates complete Solidity source units under explicit synthetic Arc
ownership. Synthetic ownership exists only to isolate parser, source, contract,
function, binding, and sink feasibility.

The prototype supports these bounded source families:

- exact `block.prevrandao`;
- one whitelisted direct cast of exact `block.prevrandao`;
- exact `block.difficulty` only with separately supplied Paris-or-later EVM-target
  evidence;
- exact inline assembly `prevrandao()` assigned to one local target;
- one exact same-function single-assignment local binding.

The prototype supports these bounded behavior sinks:

- exact array selection using `% <sameCollection>.length`;
- bounded authorization comparison;
- exact ordering derivation through `keccak256(abi.encode(...))` with one exact
  source argument and at least one non-source argument;
- exact source observation and zero-compatibility checks as safe, non-reportable
  states.

## 4. Fail-closed boundaries

The prototype rejects or marks unsupported:

- comments, strings, bare `PREVRANDAO`, and `mixHash` names as opcode sources;
- malformed or unsupported Solidity syntax;
- missing parser ranges;
- arbitrary arithmetic or function-call transformations before a sink;
- arbitrary wrappers around an otherwise supported keccak ordering expression;
- indirect collection-length ownership;
- reassignment, compound assignment, unary mutation, shadowing, two-hop aliases,
  branch joins, loops, cross-function flow, and cross-contract flow;
- transformed use of an exact Solidity local or assembly target;
- nested Yul functions and inline assembly `difficulty()`;
- assembly targets mutated after exact `prevrandao()` assignment;
- `block.difficulty` without exact EVM-target evidence.

Unsupported cases never become R3-A candidates.

## 7. Parser and dependency conclusion

`@solidity-parser/parser@0.20.2` represented the required contract, function,
statement, expression, inline-assembly, and source-range shapes for this bounded
research surface.

That is sufficient to prove parser feasibility, but it does not authorize a
production dependency.

Before a production Build decision, C09-D must separately review:

- exact dependency placement and version pinning;
- package-size and supply-chain cost;
- parser grammar/version support policy;
- malformed-source and unsupported-node behavior;
- caching and duplicate-execution behavior;
- compatibility-shell and public-ID migration.

The Solidity Standard JSON AST retained in C09-R2 remains the official comparator
for selected fixtures. R3-A does not replace that comparator.

## 8. Known research limitations

This prototype is intentionally not a general Solidity analysis platform.

It does not implement:

- project-wide import resolution;
- compiler invocation at runtime;
- general CFG, SSA, taint, or data-flow analysis;
- general Yul interpretation;
- cross-file, cross-function, or cross-contract dependency proof;
- arbitrary expression normalization;
- general sink discovery;
- deployment discovery or Arc network ownership;
- runtime finding construction or reporting.

The prototype uses bounded AST traversal plus exact node-shape predicates. Any
shape outside the reviewed contract fails closed.
